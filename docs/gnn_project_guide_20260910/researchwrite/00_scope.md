# Scope

## Audience and purpose

This is a Chinese learning guide for the project owner. It explains what the current turn-level GNN is, where its data comes from, how to read every major metric and plot, and how EXP16--EXP18 fit into the final online identification pipeline.

## Epistemic boundary

- Current evidence is synthetic and model-level.
- EXP18 is a forward surrogate, not yet a measurement-to-parameter online inverse estimator.
- No COMSOL or hardware measurement truth is claimed.
- Numerical metrics must be reported with their exact units/transforms.
